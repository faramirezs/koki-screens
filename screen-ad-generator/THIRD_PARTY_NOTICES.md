# Third-party notices

Everything vendored under `vendor/` is committed for offline rendering. Check with:

```sh
shasum -a 256 vendor/gsap.min.js vendor/fonts/*.woff2
```

## Chromium and ffmpeg

Not distributed. Both are located at runtime: Chromium via `PUPPETEER_EXECUTABLE_PATH`,
the Playwright cache, or `PATH`; ffmpeg via `PATH`. ffmpeg builds differ in licence
(GPL vs LGPL) depending on the distribution, which is one more reason not to vendor it.

## Example content

`examples/ko-kitchen/` reproduces the real Ko Kitchen brand (name, logo, palette, dish
names, allergen codes, membership tiers) for a working demonstration. The dish **prices**
there are illustrative. `examples/*/photos/*.jpg` are synthetic images generated by
`tests/make-fixtures.py` - they are stand-ins with no photographic rights attached.
