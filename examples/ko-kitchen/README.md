# Example: ko-kitchen

The real brand of the deli this skill was built for: Ko Kitchen, Harzer Str. 39,
12059 Berlin. This example exists to prove the pipeline against a **fully declared
brand** (palette override path) instead of palette extraction, and to exercise the
promo loop.

    node scripts/adkit.mjs build \
      --content examples/ko-kitchen/content.json \
      --brand   examples/ko-kitchen/brand.json \
      --photos  examples/ko-kitchen/photos \
      --out     out/ko-kitchen \
      --matting none

`--matting none` keeps the build at about 90 s: the photos are JPEGs, so the default
`--matting auto` falls back to BiRefNet at about 90 s per photo and the promo overlay uses
the flat frame instead of a cutout. Pass pre-cut PNGs with `--matting alpha` when you want
the cutout.

## Palette

`brand.json` pins the palette that was designed and contrast-audited for this brand:

| token | hex | on | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bg` | `#282F23` | — | dark green ground |
| `surface` | `#1D231A` | text `#FFFFFF` | 16.06 |
| `accent` | `#FF7D00` | surface `#1D231A` | 6.26 |
| `price` | `#FF7D00` | surface `#1D231A` | 6.26 |
| `textMuted` | `#B5BEA0` | surface `#1D231A` | 8.29 |

Because every colour is declared, extraction is skipped for this example. Extraction
is still the fallback whenever a colour is missing.

A successful build still prints warnings, and that is expected: leading tighter than the
font's ink box, and text pairs between the 4.5:1 floor and the 7:1 preferred ratio. They
are advisory. `report.ok` is the pass/fail signal.

## Promo

The promo tier carries the real membership offer: 47 € for 6 dishes, 69 € for 10
dishes per month. `promo.terms` is deliberately **absent** — minimum contract term,
notice period and validity were never confirmed, and the generator will not invent
them. Add them to `content.json` when the operator confirms; they render in the legal
strip at 24 px minimum.

## Fixture warning

`photos/*.jpg` are synthetic stand-ins generated by `tests/make-fixtures.py`, and the
dish **prices in `content.json` are illustrative** — the operator's real prices are not
published yet. The dish names, descriptions and allergen codes are the real ones from
the printed board. Replace the photos and the prices before publishing.
